## Job Description

Faculty members are expected to:

- Conduct high-quality scholarship and publish work in prestigious law reviews.
- Teach a variety of introductory and advanced business law courses.
- Teach courses including **Law, Business and Sustainability** and **Environmental Law**.
- Develop new courses.
- Provide service to the Department, School, and College.
- Teach in multiple modalities, including in-person, online, and hybrid formats.
- Participate in Zicklin programs offered on campus and with global partners.

The Department describes the faculty environment as research- and teaching-focused, vibrant, supportive, and collegial.

## Qualifications

Applicants must:

- Hold a **JD degree or a terminal law degree from an ABA-accredited U.S. law school**, **or** be **admitted to practice law in the United States**.
- Have an excellent academic record.
- Have a record of scholarly publication.
- Have a rigorous research agenda.
- Be committed to teaching business law at the undergraduate and graduate levels.

The Department of Law **strongly prefers** candidates with expertise in areas such as:

- Environmental law
- Law and sustainability
- ESG (environmental, social, and governance) regulation
- Climate law
- Related areas of environmental law and sustainability

## Application Materials

Applicants should submit:

1. **Curriculum Vitae (CV)**
2. **Research statement**
3. **Statement of teaching philosophy**

The teaching statement should include, among other topics, the applicant's commitment to and experience with teaching students in a campus environment with a substantial population of students who are among the **first generation of their family to attend a college or university**.

Candidates invited for an interview will also be required to submit:

- **CUNY Application Part 1**
- Names and contact information for **three references**

## About Baruch College and Zicklin School of Business

Baruch College is located in Manhattan's historic Gramercy/Flatiron district, between Midtown and Wall Street. The institution highlights professional opportunities to interact with the legal, banking and finance, technology, and government sectors.

The Zicklin School of Business describes itself as New York City's premier public business school, with nationally ranked programs including BBA and MBA programs, specialized MS degrees, and executive programs.

The Department of Law offers courses to undergraduate and graduate business students in subjects including:

- Introduction to Business Law
- Business Organizations
- Law, Business and Sustainability
- Environmental Law
- Financial Technology (Fintech)
- Financial Regulation
- Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- Immigration Law
- Law and Legal Theory

The Zicklin School of Business is accredited by **AACSB International** and is described in the announcement as among the largest schools of business in the United States.
