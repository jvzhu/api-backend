## Seminar Topic

The seminar will focus on **variations between Edo-period woodblock-printed editions**.

This topic concerns differences and variations among editions of books printed from woodblocks during the Edo period, with relevance to Japanese bibliographical studies, textual scholarship, rare books, and the study of historical Japanese print culture.

## Academic Relevance

This seminar is particularly relevant to scholars working on:

- Japanese literature and literary history
- Edo-period textual culture
- Japanese book history and bibliography
- Woodblock printing and textual transmission
- Kotenseki (Japanese classical and early-modern manuscripts and printed books)
- Materiality and variation in historical editions
- Japanese studies and East Asian textual scholarship

Because the seminar is conducted in Japanese and focuses on specialist bibliographical and textual issues, it may be especially useful for researchers with Japanese-language research training and an interest in premodern or early-modern Japanese textual materials.
