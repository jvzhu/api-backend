## Academic Topic

The seminar focuses on the **resilience of socialist law**, examined through the specific case of the **Polish law of obligations**.

The topic is particularly relevant to research concerning:

- comparative law;
- legal history;
- socialist and post-socialist legal systems;
- Polish law;
- law of obligations / private law;
- comparative and transnational legal studies.
