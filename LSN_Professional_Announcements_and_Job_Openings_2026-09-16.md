## ESSEC Business School — Postdoctoral Researcher

### Job Description & Qualification

**Postdoctoral Researcher (21 Months)**  
**Starting date:** November 1, 2026  
**Deadline for applying:** October 9, 2026  
**Department:** Department of Law, Political Science and Society  
**Principal Investigator:** Professor Tommaso Ramus

### Research Project

**Leveraging cybersecurity to detect illegal behavior and enforce data protection policies in the agricultural industry**

ESSEC Business School is one of Europe’s leading business schools (#9 on the FT European Business School ranking) with campuses in Greater Paris, Singapore, and Morocco. Being the first AACSB-accredited business school outside North America, it offers BBA, MSc, Advanced Masters, MBA, Executive MBA, and Ph.D. programs in business administration.

Founded in 1907, ESSEC has a long-standing tradition of humanist values and aims to prepare students for creating and running companies and organizations that can have a positive impact on the world. It is continuously recognized as the number one business school in France in its commitment to environmental sustainability and social change.

ESSEC Business School holds the HR Excellence in Research award. As part of its ongoing HRS4R (Human Resources Strategy for Researchers) commitment, ESSEC implements a recruitment policy aligned with the principles of OTM-R (Open, Transparent, and Merit-Based Recruitment).

### Position Description

- **Job Title:** Postdoctoral Researcher (Postdoc)
- **European Research Profile:** R2 (Recognised Researcher)
- **Research Project:** Leveraging cybersecurity to detect illegal behavior and enforce data protection policies in the agricultural industry
- **Principal Investigator (PI):** Tommaso Ramus
- **Location:** Cergy Pontoise, France
- **Contract Type:** Full-time fixed-term contract (CDD)
- **Duration:** 21 months
- **Start Date:** November 1, 2026
- **Gross Monthly Salary:** €2,400

### Project Background

The project is funded by the European Union within **Horizon Europe – the Framework Program for Research and Innovation (2021–2027)**. It brings together different institutional actors from across Europe and draws on interdisciplinary research in management, biotechnology, and genomics to investigate how cybersecurity can help detect illegal practices, with a particular focus on the illegal trade in vegetable seeds in Europe.

Specifically, the project seeks to improve customs and supply chain security by strengthening the prevention, detection, and deterrence of illegal activities involving the movement of goods across EU borders. ESSEC’s specific role in the project concerns the analysis of industry characteristics that facilitate or constrain illegal behavior, with particular attention to how the power, authority, and structural positions of different industry actors shape such behavior.

### Mission of the Project

The postdoctoral researcher will be involved in the following activities:

1. A systematic review of scientific and professional literature addressing the illegal reproduction of plant material protected by intellectual property rights (IPRs). Disciplines considered will include policy-making, applied ethics, and organizational studies.
2. Development of a database to map the main categories of illegal behaviors in the industry.
3. Interviews with key stakeholders across the agricultural and seed value chain, including farmers, nurseries, agronomists, and sector associations.

The interviews will explore:

- Perceived economic and practical incentives for engaging in illegal practices (e.g., cost savings, competitive pressure).
- Organizational and market factors (e.g., limited availability of authorized material, fragmented supply chains).
- Ethical and cultural dimensions (e.g., perception of low harm, normalization of illicit practices).

### Requirements

- **Profile:** Early-career scholar with a strong research orientation, intellectual curiosity, and advanced training in qualitative research.
- **Degree:** Candidate holds or is close to finishing a PhD in one of the related fields (organizational theory, public administration, or sociology) and shows a clear interest in questions at the nexus of public policy, ethics, and regulation.
- **Technical Skills:** Proven capacity to collect and analyze qualitative data.
- **Language Skills:** Professional proficiency in English (both written and spoken).
- **Location:** Candidate must reside in France and will work from the campus of Cergy.

### Eligibility Criteria

Candidates can be from any nationality. Equal opportunity and diversity for all applicants. In accordance with the ESSEC hiring policy promoting diversity, this position is accessible to people with disabilities.

## University of Iowa — Dean, College of Law

### Job Description & Qualification

The University of Iowa invites inquiries, nominations, and applications for the position of **Dean of the College of Law**.

The College of Law is one of the nation’s premier public law schools. Its influence reaches across the nation and around the world through the work of ambitious students, productive faculty and staff, and talented alumni. Iowa Law’s next dean will be positioned to move the school forward by creating new and innovative opportunities to learn, teach, and serve.

The dean will develop a clear and compelling vision for Iowa Law’s next chapter, providing leadership in matters related to planning, curriculum, scholarship, and finances. The dean will engage with faculty, staff, students, alumni, the legal community, and state and federal governmental officials to advance the goals of the College of Law and the university.

The successful candidate must possess a **JD or its equivalent** and must have a record of excellence in scholarship, teaching, and service that meets the standards for appointment to the Iowa Law faculty as a full professor with tenure. The candidate must possess a substantial record of leadership over time in a professional setting, with a strong preference given to candidates who possess substantial leadership experience in the legal academy. The successful candidate will demonstrate a capacity and readiness to advance the College of Law through fundraising and building external partnerships. The successful candidate will also demonstrate an ability to advance the university’s philosophy and practice in the areas of access and opportunity.

### University Context

Situated in a lively college town, the University of Iowa is one of the nation’s leading public research universities, with more than 30,000 students from 100-plus countries and all 50 states. Founded in 1847, it is the State of Iowa’s oldest institution of higher education and is located alongside the picturesque Iowa River in Iowa City.

A member of the **Association of American Universities (AAU)** since 1909 and the **Big Ten Conference** since 1899, the university’s distinctions range from the prestigious Iowa Writers’ Workshop to one of the largest and most acclaimed medical centers in the country.

### College of Law Highlights

The College of Law is among the most distinguished academic units in the state’s flagship university, with a legacy of excellence spanning more than 150 years.

The announcement highlights:

- A dynamic learning environment and a collegial community.
- A broad curriculum with highly regarded faculty scholarship and instruction.
- A premier, comprehensive research library.
- Four well-known law journals, including the nationally ranked *Iowa Law Review* and *Journal of Corporation Law*.
- A strong legal writing program led by seven full-time faculty members.
- A Writing Center described as the first of its kind at any law school.
- Robust field placement, practicum, simulation, and live-client clinic opportunities.
- Law Clinic practice groups including federal criminal defense, immigration, technology and surveillance, federal civil rights, and the Community Empowerment Law Project.

### Degree Programs

In addition to the **Juris Doctor (JD), Master of Laws (LLM), and Doctor of Juridical Science (SJD)** degrees, Iowa Law offers:

- Advanced-standing JD for lawyers trained abroad.
- JD/MHA.
- JD/MPH.
- JD/MBA.
- JD/MD.
- JD/MS Business Analytics.
- JD/Master of Finance.
- JD/MA Philosophy.
- JD/MS Library and Information Science.

### Alumni and Advancement

Iowa Law enjoys a network of more than **11,000 alumni** spanning the state, nation, and world.

The Iowa Law School Foundation, with an endowment of nearly **$100 million**, and the University of Iowa Center for Advancement serve as partners to the dean and College of Law in fundraising, alumni relations, endowment management, and program support.

Candidates will be subject to a criminal background and credential check.

## CUNEF Universidad — Assistant Professor (Tenure-track)

### Job Description & Qualification

The Department of Law at **CUNEF University** invites applications for one or more **Assistant Professor tenure-track positions**. The appointment will begin on **September 1, 2027**.

The department seeks candidates with an original and relevant research agenda and the potential to build a strong record of high-impact publications. Candidates should also demonstrate a commitment to excellence and flexibility in teaching.

### Areas of Expertise

While the department welcomes and encourages applications from excellent candidates across all fields, it has particular needs in:

- Public Law
- International and/or European Tax Law
- International Commercial Law
- International Company Law
- Banking and Financial Regulation
- Labour Law — **fluency in Spanish is a must for this area**

### Application Procedure

**Application deadline:** October 15, 2026, at 11:59 PM CET

Submit:

- Cover letter (maximum 2 pages).
- Curriculum vitae, including a full list of publications.
- One representative article or sample research paper.
- Contact details of two referees.

Longlisted candidates will be invited to a video interview. Shortlisted candidates will be invited to present a recent publication or a work in progress on campus.

### Minimum Requirements

- **PhD in Law** or completed by the start of the position.
- University teaching experience or demonstrated potential to teach at university level.
- Publications in internationally recognized journals or demonstrated potential to publish in them.
- Spanish is not required at this stage, though successful candidates are expected to acquire it as the working language of the department.

### What They Offer

- Competitive salary, in line with top-ranked European academic institutions.
- Generous seed fund to cover conference, research, and travel costs.
- Full access to public health services through membership in the National Insurance Scheme, plus a subsidised private health insurance plan.
- Professional and stimulating working environment within a diverse, innovative, and multidisciplinary scholarly community.
- The opportunity to live in one of Europe’s most vibrant capitals.
