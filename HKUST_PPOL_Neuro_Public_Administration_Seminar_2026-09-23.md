## About the Seminar

Public management and policy research has experienced substantial growth in experimental research, particularly over the past decade. The email notes that this work has been dominated by survey experiments, which can help investigate attitudes, intentions, and perceptions but may not provide objective measurements of cognitive and behavioral effects.

The seminar introduces **Neuro Public Administration (NPA)** as an approach that employs neuroscientific theories, methods, and concepts to better understand administrative behavior. Professor Bert George will discuss:

- Why Neuro Public Administration is needed.
- The core foundations of NPA.
- Specific examples of how to conduct NPA research as a public management and policy scholar.
- Findings from experiments conducted in his laboratory.
- How neuroscience can be used to investigate the biological foundations of behavior among policymakers, public managers, citizens, and other stakeholders.

## About the Speaker

Bert George is a Professor in the Department of Public and International Affairs and Associate Dean of the College of Liberal Arts and Social Sciences at the City University of Hong Kong.

His public management research focuses especially on:

- Strategic planning in public organizations
- Red tape and administrative burden
- Neuro Public Administration

He is **Co-Editor in Chief of *Public Management Review*** and a former editor of *Public Administration Review*.

According to the invitation email, he has published more than 60 articles, primarily in leading public administration journals, and has received several awards, including recognition from the American Society for Public Administration. The email also states that he is included on the Stanford top 2% Most Cited Scientists list and that his work has been cited by organizations including the OECD, World Bank, U.S. White House, European Commission, and governments and nonprofits internationally.
